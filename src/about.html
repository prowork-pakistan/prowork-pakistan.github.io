About Prowork

A coworking space in DHA Phase 2, Islamabad, right next to Giga Mall. Desks by the day or the month, and private rooms for teams.

Our story

Your corner of DHA2.

Prowork is a coworking space for freelancers, startups, remote teams, agencies and students. You get a desk, fast internet, parking in the building and a quiet hall, without the cost and hassle of setting up an office of your own.

Prowork started in Bahria Town Phase 8 as a workspace for IT teams. Today we are in DHA Phase 2, next to Giga Mall, and the space is open to anyone who needs a good place to work, whether that is one day a week or every day.

You will notice the arched nooks around the hall. They gave us our look: an arch is a doorway. You arrive, you step in, and you have your own corner of the room.
