Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

ProWork (“we”, “us”) runs a coworking space at Plaza No 9, Street A26 Tulip Road, Sector A, DHA Phase 2, Islamabad. This policy explains what information we collect through prowork.com.pk and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you fill in a form on this site, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, email address, the plan you are interested in and your message. Forms are delivered to us by email through Web3Forms, a form-handling service.

When you message us on WhatsApp or call us, we receive your phone number and whatever you share in the conversation. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

Information collected automatically

  • Google Analytics helps us understand how people use the site, for example which pages are visited and roughly where visitors are.
  • Meta Pixel helps us measure our Facebook and Instagram ads.

These services use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as your device, browser and IP address.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange visits.
  • To run and improve our coworking space and this website.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out browser add-on, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings. You can also ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you have sent us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep normal business records, and then delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
